# Five-film poster placement preview

Five remote promotional poster references were inspected on official Disney movie pages on November 12, 2026: Finding Nemo, Up, Frozen, Inside Out and Coco. Exact CDN URLs and the source page for each are recorded in src/Posters.jsx. Posters are fetched from Disney's image CDN in the browser; no image files are downloaded into or redistributed with the repository. Images and characters remain copyrighted by their respective owners; source attribution is not a claim of an open licence or unrestricted reuse. This is a local layout preview, not clearance for publishing a 95-film poster collection. Check image-specific permissions before expanded/public use. Failed loads display an explicit fallback rather than a fabricated poster. No movie data or annotation evidence is derived from poster appearance.
